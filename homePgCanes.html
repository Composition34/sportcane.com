-The Problem

Traditional canes
were never built
for recovery.

  1. Poor Posture Old canes force you to hunch, compounding the strain they were meant to relieve.
  2. Joint Strain Vertical loading drives force straight into the joints that need protecting.
  3. Temporary Support They prop you up in the moment, then leave your body to fend for itself.
  4. No Recovery Assistance Nothing about their design helps you heal, retrain, or get stronger.

Millions stay dependent longer because traditional canes only help people walk - not heal.
